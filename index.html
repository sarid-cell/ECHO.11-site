Why this exists

More calm, made by a human and two AIs.

ECHO.11 exists to bring a little more calm into people’s days: sound you can press play on, and science you can trust. It is built by me, Shira, with two AI agents, Claude and Grok. They write code, check that every page works, and both make the videos. I decide what to make, upload every change by hand, and check it again. Right now I am reading Joe Dispenza’s Becoming Supernatural. Its breathing practices inspired the Breath Lab videos, made to help people reconnect with themselves. Where a claim goes beyond the research, this site says so.
