The Bands

The brainwave bands, explained

Five frequency bands, what the research says about each, and what it does not. Every page names its sources where they exist and says plainly where the evidence is thin.

0.5–4 Hz · Sleep

Delta waves

The slowest band, and the signature of deep sleep. Not something you enter while awake.
4–8 Hz · Drift

Theta waves

Between sleep and waking. It appears in drowsiness and in hard concentration, which is why one feeling does not describe it.
8–13 Hz · Calm

Alpha waves

The rhythm of relaxed wakefulness, and the band with the most consistent evidence behind it. The 10 Hz session sits in the middle of it.
13–30 Hz · Alert

Beta waves

The background rhythm of being awake. More of it is not better, and no session here uses it.
30–100 Hz · Focus

Gamma waves

The fastest band, and the hardest to record cleanly. The 40 Hz session sits low in it.
