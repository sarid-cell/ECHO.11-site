Shira Sarid

How your brain rewires, in twenty seconds a day

I'm Shira Sarid. I write short, research-backed notes on neuroplasticity, perception and memory, and on what AI is teaching us about our own heads.

I'm not a neuroscientist. I read the studies, mostly from the last three years, and turn one finding at a time into something you can feel in under a minute: a Reel, a short story about a character called Alex, or a single tool worth saving.

I live in Israel, think in Hebrew, and publish in English. ECHO.11, the free sound sessions on this site, is where it started.
